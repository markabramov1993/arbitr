## Description
RustChain’s Proof-of-Antiquity design does not rely on a claimed CPU model alone. Its protocol combines six behavioral hardware signals — clock drift, cache timing, SIMD behavior, thermal response, instruction-path jitter, and anti-emulation heuristics — so spoofing has to reproduce multiple physical characteristics at once.
