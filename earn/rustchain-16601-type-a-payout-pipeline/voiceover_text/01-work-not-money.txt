A bounty can show a reward, and a contributor can finish the work, but neither fact means the money is already in a wallet. RustChain's bounty tooling separates several stages. First comes the contribution: a bug report, review, content package, or other deliverable. Then a gate or maintainer decides whether the work is actually eligible. Only after that does the payout layer try to create a transfer. That separation sounds boring, but it prevents the most dangerous accounting mistake in bounty work: treating a sticker price as cash before anyone has accepted it.
